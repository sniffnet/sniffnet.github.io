**_Welcome to the Sniffnet Wiki!_**
</div>


If you are here, there are good chances you are interested in learning how to use Sniffnet to its fullest potential.<br>
This is the right place!

Despite one of Sniffnet's cornerstones is to be usable in a **straightforward** way,
the constant addition of new features is leading to considerable **evolution of the app**,
introducing the need for users to have a **reference manual**. <br>
The Sniffnet Wiki wants to address this concern.

***

For your convenience, the wiki is divided into **different sections**, each one targeting specific aspects of the application:

- [**_Getting started_**](/wiki/getting-started/): the first section wants to guide new users through the installation of the app; accurate directions are provided based on the target operating system

- [**_Set up the analysis_**](/wiki/set-up-the-analysis/): it provides instructions and tips to properly start a new network traffic capture

- [**_Internet traffic monitoring_**](/wiki/internet-traffic-monitoring/): core part of the wiki, it aims to describe how Sniffnet makes it possible to obtain useful insights about Internet traffic

- [**_Advanced features_**](/wiki/advanced-features/): it covers additional topics needed to completely master all the application functionalities

Moreover, there's also an [**_FAQ page_**](/wiki/faq/) answering some of the most common questions about the application.
