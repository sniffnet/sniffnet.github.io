---
title: "Thumbnail mode"
---
Sniffnet provides valuable insights about your network traffic, but sometimes you just can't
dedicate a whole screen to the application. <br>
In such cases, you can switch the view to **thumbnail mode** by clicking on the dedicated icon in the app's header.

<div align="center">
<img alt="" width="80%" src="/assets/img/wiki/clip_thumbnail_icon.png">
</div>

_Thumbnail mode_ is a **compact view** of the app that can be moved to any corner of the screen, allowing you to keep an eye on your traffic while performing other tasks. <br>

<div align="center">
<img alt="" width="80%" src="/assets/img/wiki/thumbnail.png">
</div>

The thumbnail includes **live charts** about the total exchanged data and the traffic rate, similar to the ones present in the _Overview_ page,
and some of the most recently observed **network hosts** and **services**. <br>
You'll continue **receiving notifications** even in thumbnail mode, and a badge will appear to signal the number of unread notifications.

You can switch back to the **full view** at any time by clicking on the _expand_ icon in the thumbnail's header.
